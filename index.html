DrugBank-in-3D: a 3D drug–target structural dataset

Development of a comprehensive structural resource to support molecular modeling, structure-based drug discovery, and downstream AI-driven research.

  • Generated, predicted, and validated more than 37,000 biomolecular structures.
  • Built automated Python pipelines for data retrieval, structure prediction, quality assessment, and computational analysis.
  • Integrated AlphaFold2/3, AlphaFold-Multimer, Boltz, SWISS-MODEL, and MODELLER with docking, refinement, and simulation workflows.
  • Contributed to greater than 99% structural coverage across major DrugBank categories, as reported in the CV.
